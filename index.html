Loss Landscape Golf

The curve is a loss function. The ball is your parameter. Sink it into the global minimum in as few steps as you can — and make it come to rest there.

Controls

  • Drag away from the ball like a slingshot, then release to shoot.
  • Or hold ← → to charge and release. R restarts.
  • Charge power is your learning rate: too small won't clear the ridge, too large overshoots.

Terrain

Local minimumComfortable, but wrong. You need momentum to escape.
Saddle sandVanishing gradient, huge friction. Get stuck and you barely move.
IceAlmost no damping — what an oversized learning rate feels like.
lr decayRoll too long and damping ramps up, so every shot converges.
